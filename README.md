# BarkyDogMap
Privacy Policy for Barky Dog Map

1. Data Collection and Usage
Barky Dog Map is designed to be a local-first application. We do not collect, transmit, distribute, or sell your personal data. All map pins, walk histories, and text descriptions are saved strictly to the local SQLite database on your personal device.

2. Camera Permission
The app requests access to your device's Camera (android.permission.CAMERA) exclusively to allow you to take photos of waypoints and hazards during your walks. These photos are saved directly to your device's local internal storage. We do not upload, view, or analyze these photos.

3. Location Permission
The app requests Location access (including background location) to accurately track your walking route and calculate distances. This location data is processed locally on your device to draw the map route and is not transmitted to any external servers.

4. Data Retention
Your data remains on your device until you manually uninstall the app, clear the app data, or use the in-app feature to delete specific records.
